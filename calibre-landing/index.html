The Calibre method, by Daftar

The Method

Four steps from opinion to a written standard.

Each step leaves a record, so the reasoning behind the recommendation can be checked later.

1

Define

Write the role standard with you before any finalist is read.

2

Read

Two readers score each finalist independently against a role-specific standard. Finalists are presented in entry order.

3

Compare

Put the notes side by side and make the disagreement visible.

4

Calibrate

Resolve or name the disagreement in one written recommendation, with the reasons and risks named.

FAQ

What it is, and what it isn’t

Is this recruitment?

No. You source the shortlist. We review it against a written role standard and hand back a memo. We do not source or place candidates.

Does Calibre choose the candidate?

No. The memo gives a recommendation with reasons, risks and missing evidence. Your team makes the decision.

Why finance roles only?

The review depends on finance judgment. Reading whether someone owned a close, designed a control or made a technical call is the same work Daftar does on financial statements.

Does it replace our interviewers?

No. Your panel still interviews. Calibre gives them a shared standard and specific follow-up questions.
