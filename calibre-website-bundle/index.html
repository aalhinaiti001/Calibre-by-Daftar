The Calibre method, by Daftar

The Method

Define good before you look, then read against it.

Four steps move a team from gut feel to a shared, evidence based standard, so two reviewers reading the same candidate start from the same facts.

1

Read

Read every CV and profile the same way.

2

Score

Decide what "good" looks like for the role before you evaluate.

3

Compare

Find where reviewers disagree, and exactly why.

4

Calibrate

Align the team on evidence, not preference, then make the call.
