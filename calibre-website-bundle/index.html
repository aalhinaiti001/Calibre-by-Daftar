01 · The Problem

The Hidden Cost of Hiring Noise

Two recruiters or interviewers can assess the same candidate and reach very different conclusions. This creates inconsistent shortlists, slow hiring decisions, unclear interview feedback, and too much reliance on gut feeling.

The result? Weak evidence behind hiring or rejection decisions. Teams can't explain why they chose one candidate over another. Decisions don't hold up under scrutiny. And the best candidates sometimes fall through the cracks.

✕
Inconsistent shortlists — the same CV gets different scores from different people
✕
Slow hiring decisions — unclear criteria mean more back-and-forth
✕
Unclear interview feedback — interviewers use different language, different standards
✕
Too much gut feeling — decisions rest on intuition, not evidence
✕
Weak defensibility — you can't explain why you hired or rejected someone
02 · The Method

Define Good Before You Look — Then Read Against It

Calibre gives hiring teams a structured way to define what "good" looks like before they screen, interview, and decide. It is a practical hiring-decision diagnostic and calibration service designed to improve the evidence behind hiring decisions.

We help teams move from gut feel to a shared, evidence-based framework. The result is faster decisions, clearer feedback, and candidates evaluated on what actually matters for the role.

Not

A personality test. Not another ATS. Not interviewer training.

Instead

Calibre does not predict personality or automate judgment. It improves the quality of human judgment by making evaluation criteria explicit, comparable, and defensible.
