How it works

A clear path from first call to final handover.

Four steps that keep the work focused and stop scope creep.

Step 01

Pin down the issue

We talk through your problem on a short call to check we're the right fit.

Step 02

Set the scope

You get a fixed-scope, fixed-fee proposal: what's in, what's out, what you'll receive.

Step 03

Do the work

We build, check, and draft the files ourselves — nothing outsourced.

Step 04

Hand over and support

You get your files plus a plain-English briefing, ready for your board, lenders, or auditors.

What clients receive

Clear outputs, not vague progress.

Output changes by job, but the standard holds: clear, easy to read, usable by your team.

01
Written output

A memo, comments, or summary that explains the result in plain words.

02
Working file

Where it helps, you get the file behind the work — not a black box.

03
Next-step clarity

If something should go to an auditor, tax advisor, or specialist, we flag it early.

Why this helps

A clear process cuts friction. You know the scope, the fee, and the next step at every stage.
