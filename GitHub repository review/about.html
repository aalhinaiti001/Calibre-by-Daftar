About

Senior finance help, without the big-firm pyramid.

How the practice runs, why it's built this way, and who it fits.

Who this is for

Growing companies that need senior finance help.

A fit when a founder, CFO, or finance lead needs real help with an important file, number, or accounting issue.

  • Founder-led companies preparing for growth
  • Finance teams handling new reporting pressure

How the practice works

Built to cut noise. You talk directly to the person doing the work, on one clear problem.

  • Senior-led work from start to finish
  • Clear engagement boundaries
  • Less handoff, less confusion
  • Practical files your team can keep using

Recent work

Two engagements, anonymised.

Both ran fixed-fee against a set scope, with the working file behind them. Client names on request, subject to confidentiality.

KSA construction JV · SAR 800M revenue

IFRS 16 lease recomputation across 47 contracts.

The model understated the right-of-use asset by about 11%, from discount rates applied inconsistently across the equipment leases. We rebuilt the full IFRS 16 schedule from the source contracts, tied it to the audited prior year, and handed over one working file with a full audit trail.

  • Deliverable — recomputed schedule, technical memo, audit response file
  • Timeline — 3 weeks, fixed fee
  • Outcome — auditor accepted the position with no further changes
MENA healthcare group · SAR 1.2B revenue

Full financial statements and audit-readiness package.

First outside audit after three years of internal-only reporting. We built the full financial statements — cleanly linked from trial balance to notes — wrote up every policy, and prepared the audit-support file before the auditor arrived.

  • Deliverable — draft FS, notes, policy memo, PBC binder
  • Timeline — 5 weeks, fixed fee
  • Outcome — audit finished about 40% faster than the auditor's first estimate
The refusal

We protect our focus by turning down the wrong projects. Statutory audits, high-volume tax filing, government lobbying, and set-outcome legal valuations belong elsewhere. If we aren't a fit, we say so on the first call and point you to someone who is.

Independence and engagement basis

Daftar Advisory is a non-attest advisory practice. The principal holds no JACPA, SOCPA, or UAE Ministry of Economy audit licence and does not perform statutory audit, review, or agreed-upon-procedures work; where an attest signature is needed, we refer it out. Every job runs under a written engagement letter with fixed scope, fee, and timeline, and a written non-attest disclaimer.
