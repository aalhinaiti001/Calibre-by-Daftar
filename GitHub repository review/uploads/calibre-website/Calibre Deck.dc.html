01 · The problem

The hidden cost of hiring noise.

Two interviewers can assess the same candidate and reach opposite conclusions. Evidence stays weak, and the decisions can't be explained or defended.

✕Inconsistent shortlists — the same CV gets different scores
✕Slow decisions — unclear criteria mean more back-and-forth
✕Unclear feedback — different language, different standards
✕Too much gut feeling — intuition standing in for evidence
✕Weak defensibility — you can't explain who you hired, or why
02 · The method

Define good before you look — then read against it.

1

Read

Standardize how CVs are interpreted, so two reviewers start from the same facts.

2

Score

Define what "good" means for the role before a first impression forms.

3

Compare

Find where reviewers disagree — and exactly why.

4

Calibrate

Align the team on evidence, not preference, and recommend.
