Ethics and values

What we stand for.

Four values guide every job.

Value 01

Explicit boundaries

We agree the limits of the work up front — no hidden assumptions, no surprise fees.

Value 02

Technical candor

If a treatment won't survive the audit, we say so early and offer options that will hold up.

Value 03

Model transparency

Spreadsheets are for doing the math, not locking up your data. Our models are open, easy to trace, and yours to take over.

Value 04

Applied pragmatism

Accounting should help you decide, not sit in a theory silo. We give you answers you can use, not dense memos.

Letter from the founder

A short note from Ahmad.

Daftar Advisory is for clients who want serious finance work without extra layers. Most companies don't need a big advisory machine — they need a clear answer, a strong file, and someone who stands behind it.

That's what this practice gives: direct, thoughtful, practical. If there's a fit, the work feels easy. If there isn't, you hear it quickly.

Ahmad Alhinaiti
