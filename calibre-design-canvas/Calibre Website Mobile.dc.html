The Calibre method

Four steps from opinion to evidence.

01

Read

A role-specific reading guide tells every reviewer exactly what signal to look for.

02

Score

Structured scorecards convert judgment into comparable, defensible evidence.

03

Compare

Scores line up side by side, so disagreement surfaces immediately.

04

Calibrate

A facilitated session resolves the gaps into one decision.
