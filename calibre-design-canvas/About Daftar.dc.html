The firm

A register, not a rolodex.

Daftar — daf·tar, the ledger or register — is named for the discipline at the heart of the practice: writing decisions down, in a form that holds up. We founded the firm to serve leaders across Jordan, the GCC, and the wider MENA region who are tired of advice that evaporates the moment the engagement ends.

We work in small, senior teams. Every engagement leaves you with a working artifact — a model, a method, a memo — not a binder that goes on a shelf. Our job is to make your reasoning legible: to you, to your board, and to whoever inherits the decision after you.

Calibre, our hiring-decision diagnostic, is the first productized expression of that belief — the same rigor we bring to capital, turned on the way teams evaluate people.

What we do

Three lines of practice.

01

Decision advisory

Structuring the high-stakes calls — capital allocation, investment, restructuring — so the rationale is explicit and the record is defensible.

02

Diagnostics & method

Turning recurring judgment calls into repeatable methods — scorecards, review protocols, calibration. Calibre lives here.

03

Governance & records

Building the documentation layer boards and regulators expect — so decisions can be explained long after they're made.

How we work

Four principles we don't bend.

01

Write it down.

If a decision can't be written in a form that holds up, it isn't finished.

02

Senior hands only.

You get the people you met in the room — no hand-off to a junior bench.

03

Leave a tool behind.

Every engagement ends with something you can run yourself, next time.

04

Evidence over opinion.

We'd rather show the working than defend a verdict on authority.
