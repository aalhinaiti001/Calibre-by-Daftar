The Calibre method

Four steps from opinion to evidence.

01

Read

A role-specific reading guide tells every reviewer exactly what signal to look for.

02

Score

Structured scorecards convert judgment into comparable, defensible evidence.

03

Compare

Scores line up side by side, so disagreement surfaces immediately — not after the offer.

04

Calibrate

A facilitated session resolves the gaps into one decision the whole team can stand behind.

Common questions

Before you book the call.

01

Is this software or a service?

A service, delivered as a working kit. We build the scorecard, reading guide, and question bank for your role, then facilitate the calibration — you keep every artifact afterwards.

02

How long does a pilot take?

Two to three weeks for a single role, from the scoping session to the findings memo. It's fixed-scope, so timelines and cost are clear before we start.

03

Does it replace our interviewers?

No — it sharpens them. Your panel still does the interviewing and scoring; Calibre gives them a shared standard so their judgments become comparable instead of conflicting.

04

What roles does it work for?

Any role where the decision matters and the panel disagrees — from senior individual contributors to leadership hires. We build the criteria fresh each time, so it isn't tied to one function.

05

What do we need to bring?

One active or recent hire and the panel who ran it. That's enough to run the method end-to-end and see the difference on your own pipeline.
